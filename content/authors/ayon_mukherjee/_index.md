## Background

I am a Senior Research Associate in the Biostatistics Research Group at the Population Health Sciences Institute, Newcastle University, where I work on the development and application of innovative statistical methodologies for the design and analysis of clinical trials. I am also a Chartered Statistician (C.Stat.).

Prior to joining academia, I spent over 13 years in the pharmaceutical industry, holding senior statistical and methodological leadership positions at organisations including GSK, ICON, IQVIA, Merck KGaA and Eli Lilly. My industry experience includes leading methodological initiatives in clinical trial design and development, direct regulatory interactions, with a particular focus on innovative and complex clinical trial methodologies. 

## Research

My research interests include innovative clinical trial designs, adaptive designs, Dose Optimization Design methods for early oncology trials, Covariate-Adjusted Response-adaptive randomisation, Bayesian methods, Estimands and causal inference, Adaptive Designs for Master Protocols, Complex Innovative Designs and statistical methods for accelerated approval in oncology drug development. I have a particular interest in addressing methodological challenges in dose optimisation, particularly in the context of US. FDA's Project Optimus initiative, and in developing robust statistical approaches for trials involving non-proportional hazards, recurrent events, competing risks, and external data borrowing. 

My work combines methodological research, statistical innovation and practical application to clinical development. I am particularly interested in translating novel statistical methodologies into practical solutions that address real-world challenges in pharmaceutical research and regulatory decision-making. I am actively involved in international methodological collaborations, regulatory interactions, professional statistical societies and editorial activities, including serving as an Associate Editor of Pharmaceutical Statistics and Chief Guest Editor of its Project Optimus Special Issue.
