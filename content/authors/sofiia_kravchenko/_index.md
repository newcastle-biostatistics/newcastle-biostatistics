## Background

I am a PhD student in biostatistics, working on statistical methods for multi-arm clinical trials.
I am particularly interested in the mathematical theory behind trial design, and I enjoy teaching and popularising statistics and mathematics.

I joined [Newcastle University](https://www.ncl.ac.uk/) in October 2026 as part of the TMRP Doctoral Training Programme, supervised by James Wason and Helen Mossop.

Before that, I completed a Master's degree in Mathematics at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de/), where my thesis focused on mathematical modelling of HPV spread and progression to cervical cancer. I also gained industry experience in the pharmaceutical sector.

### Areas of expertise

- Bayesian methods and statistical theory
- Multi-arm clinical trials
- Pure mathematics (geometry and topology)
- Mathematical modelling in biology and medicine


## Research

My PhD focuses on improving the design and analysis of multi-arm clinical trials, with applications to the PREMISE trial.
I am particularly interested in:

1. Bayesian borrowing of information from historical or external controls;
2. Personalised multi-arm designs with shared control arms;
3. Causal methods for handling intercurrent events.
