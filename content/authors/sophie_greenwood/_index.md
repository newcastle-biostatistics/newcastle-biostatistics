## Background

I am an applied trial statistician with experience across both academia and the pharmaceutical industry.
I am a early career researcher in trial methodology, aiming to improve the statistical decision-making and evidence available to statisticians throughout clinical trials.

### Areas of expertise

- Clinical trials
- Expert elicitation 
- Patient and public involvement 
- Missing data
- Estimands

## Research

I am actively involved in the following research areas and am always open to new collaborations:

1. Missing data and retention: from practical advice to improving the data we collect on missing data;
2. Estimand implementation: improving uptake within trials;
3. PPI in statistics: exploring how patients can be involved in statistical decision-making (SDM);
4. Expert elicitation: how to capture opinions numerically to inform SDM.
